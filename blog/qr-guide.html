1. What is a QR Code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a two‑dimensional barcode that can store URLs, text, WiFi credentials, contact information (vCard), email addresses, and SMS messages. Scanned by any smartphone camera, QR codes bridge the physical and digital worlds instantly.

5. Best Practices

  • Test before printing — Scan with multiple devices
  • Use high contrast — Dark QR on light background
  • Keep quiet zone — Minimum 4‑module white border
  • Don't scale too small — Minimum 2×2 cm for print
  • Use H correction when adding logos
