1. What is PDF Merging?

PDF merging is the process of combining two or more PDF files into a single document. This is useful when you have multiple reports, scanned pages, or invoices that you want to consolidate into one file for easier sharing, printing, or archiving.

2. How to Use PixelPress Merge PDF

  1. Upload your PDFs — Click the upload area or drag & drop multiple PDF files at once.
  2. Arrange the order — Drag the file cards to reorder them. The top file becomes page 1.
  3. Click "Merge PDFs" — The tool combines all files instantly.
  4. Download — Save the merged PDF to your device.

3. Privacy & Security

All merging happens entirely in your browser using client‑side JavaScript. Your PDFs are never uploaded to any server. This means your sensitive documents remain completely private and secure on your own device.

4. Tips for Best Results

For optimal performance, merge up to 20 files at a time. If you have more, merge them in batches. All PDFs retain their original quality — the merge process is lossless.
