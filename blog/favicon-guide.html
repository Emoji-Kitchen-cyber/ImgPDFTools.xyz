A favicon is the small icon appearing in browser tabs, bookmarks, and search results. PixelPress Favicon Generator creates all standard sizes automatically — 16×16 to 512×512 — right in your browser.

📏 Standard Sizes

16×16, 32×32, 48×48, 64×64, 96×96, 128×128, 180×180 (Apple), 192×192 (Android), 512×512 (PWA). All packaged in a single ZIP download.
