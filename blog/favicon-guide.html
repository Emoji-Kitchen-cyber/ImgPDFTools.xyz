What is a Favicon?

A favicon (favorite icon) is the small icon displayed in browser tabs, bookmarks, and shortcuts. It represents your brand identity in a 16×16 or 32×32 pixel square. Modern websites need multiple sizes for different devices.

Standard Favicon Sizes

  • 16×16 – Browser tab (most common)
  • 32×32 – Taskbar / bookmarks
  • 48×48 – Windows tiles
  • 64×64 – Safari Reading List
  • 180×180 – Apple Touch Icon
  • 192×192 – Android Chrome
  • 512×512 – PWA splash screen

Implementation in HTML

Once you have your favicon.ico file, upload it to your website root and add this code inside <head>:

<link rel="icon" type="image/x-icon" href="/favicon.ico">
