Long URLs are hard to type, ugly in print, and impossible to fit in a tweet or SMS. A URL shortener converts any link into a clean, compact short URL that is easier to share — without tracking your data on a third-party server.

1. Why Shorten URLs

Short links improve click-through rates in marketing materials, make URLs readable in printed documents, fit within character limits on social platforms, and reduce errors when people type a link manually. A browser-based shortener lets you create these links with no account and no data leaving your device, since the redirect mapping is stored in your browser's localStorage.

💡 Tip: Short links generated by ImgPDFTools work only on the device and browser where they were created, since the mapping is stored locally. For links you need to share across devices, use a cloud-based shortener instead.

2. Features

  • Instant shortening — paste any URL and get a short link in one click.
  • Click tracking — see how many times each short link has been clicked.
  • Expiry dates — set a date after which the link expires and no longer redirects.
  • Custom slugs — replace the random short code with a custom word or phrase.
  • Link management — view, edit, and delete all your short links from one dashboard.

3. Step by Step

  • Open the URL shortener — no sign-up needed.
  • Paste your long URL — enter the destination you want to shorten.
  • Set options — add a custom slug or expiry date if needed.
  • Generate — click to create the short link instantly.
  • Copy and share — use the copy button to grab the short URL for sharing.

All data is stored in your browser's localStorage. No link is ever sent to an external server.

4. Tips

  • Use descriptive slugs — a link like /docs is more trustworthy to click than a random string of characters.
  • Set expiry for time-limited offers — promotional links that expire prevent confusion after a campaign ends.
  • Check click counts regularly — use the dashboard to see which links get the most traffic.
  • Back up your links — clearing browser data deletes all stored short links; export them periodically if they are important.
