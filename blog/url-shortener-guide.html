1. What is URL Shortening?

URL shortening converts a long, complex web address into a compact, easy‑to‑share link. For example, https://example.com/very/long/path?param=value becomes https://pxl.to/abc123. Short links are cleaner for social media, SMS, QR codes, and printed materials.

2. Why Use Short URLs?

4. Custom Aliases & Expiry

With custom aliases, you can create memorable links like /summer‑sale or /contact. Set an expiry date to automatically disable links after a certain period — perfect for limited‑time offers or event registrations.

5. Tracking & History

PixelPress URL Shortener stores your last 50 links in your browser's local storage. Each link shows the original URL, short URL, creation date, expiry status, and click count. Links work via hash‑based routing — no server required.

6. Batch Shortening

Need to shorten 10 URLs at once? Paste them all (one per line) into the batch input area. All URLs are shortened simultaneously and added to your history.
