1. What is OCR and How It Works

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) is a technology that converts images of text — such as scanned documents, screenshots, or photos of signs — into editable, searchable digital text. PixelPress OCR Scanner uses Tesseract.js, the most popular open‑source OCR engine, running entirely in your browser. The process involves analyzing the image, detecting text regions, recognizing individual characters, and assembling them into words and sentences.

4. Best Practices for Accurate Results

  • Use high‑resolution images — Text should be at least 12px in height for reliable recognition.
  • Ensure good lighting — Avoid shadows, glare, and uneven illumination.
  • Straighten the image — Skewed text reduces accuracy significantly.
  • Select the correct language — This dramatically improves recognition quality.
  • Pre‑process if needed — Crop to the text area, increase contrast, or convert to grayscale before uploading.
