1. What is a Color Palette?

A color palette is a set of colors used together in a design project — website, app, branding, presentation, or artwork. A well‑chosen palette creates visual harmony, reinforces brand identity, and guides user attention. Color theory provides mathematical rules (complementary, analogous, triadic, etc.) for creating harmonious combinations.

2. Color Harmony Types Explained

  • Complementary — Two colors opposite on the color wheel (e.g., blue & orange). High contrast, energetic.
  • Analogous — Three colors next to each other (e.g., blue, teal, green). Calm, cohesive.
  • Triadic — Three evenly spaced colors (e.g., red, yellow, blue). Vibrant, balanced.
  • Tetradic — Four colors forming a rectangle. Rich, complex — best with one dominant color.
  • Monochromatic — Variations of a single hue. Clean, elegant, minimal.
