1. What is a Screenshot Mockup?

A screenshot mockup places your app screen, website, or digital product inside a realistic device frame (iPhone, MacBook, browser window) against a beautiful background. This transforms a plain screenshot into a professional, shareable marketing asset — perfect for app stores, social media, presentations, and portfolios.

3. Background Options

  • Gradient: Sunset glow, Ocean breeze, Midnight dark — professional pre‑made gradients
  • Solid Color: Pick any color to match your brand

4. How to Use the Editor

  1. Upload your screenshot — Click "Choose Image" to select your screenshot file
  2. Select a frame — Choose iPhone, MacBook, Browser, or Clean Shadow
  3. Pick a background — Gradient, solid color, or custom image
  4. Adjust padding — Control the space around your screenshot
  5. Adjust shadow — Control shadow intensity for depth
  6. Export — Download as PNG, JPEG, or WebP
